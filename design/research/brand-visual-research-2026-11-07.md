## Product truth that governs the research

The product is not a push-to-talk dictation button. It is a persistent macOS expression surface: one transcript appears continuously in a narrow desktop-edge panel, is revised in place, remains available, and can be deliberately reused. The current product target specifies a 320–360 pt wide panel, transcript-first hierarchy, one discreet capture control, and a stable menu-bar entry. Therefore the logo must work independently of the panel and should not be a miniature representation of the panel.

## Visual case studies

The companion HTML board embeds the actual screenshots / official visual assets available from public sources. Each card labels whether the image is an official product source or a third-party editorial capture; third-party imagery is never represented as official brand material.

### Direct voice products

1. **Wispr Flow** — Official media kit documents a deliberately monochrome logo and provides separate product UI assets. Its desktop Flow Bar is an ephemeral dictation control; the Hub is a separate history/settings destination. Lesson: separate the compact runtime affordance from the fuller brand/home experience. Do not copy its monochrome icon or bar silhouette. Sources: [official media kit](https://wisprflow.ai/media-kit), [desktop product guide](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/5096240724-navigating-the-wispr-flow-app-desktop-ios-and-android), [third-party interface capture](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize%3Afit%3A1200/1%2ALTfpATBlKq-peE4g17uf6A.png).
2. **Superwhisper** — Official brand asset page separates wordmark, trademark, app icon and macOS screenshots; its current app icon uses a sculptural, continuous triangular loop on a dark field. Lesson: the mark can carry character without depicting a microphone or transcript. Risk: a loop/rounded triangular enclosure is a crowded abstract-tech motif and should not be borrowed. Sources: [official brand assets](https://ai.superwhisper.com/assets), [official 1024px app icon](https://ai.superwhisper.com/app-icon/1024.png), [official app screens download](https://ai.superwhisper.com/downloads/presskit/superwhisper-app-screens.png).
3. **Aqua Voice** — The product deliberately reduces the interaction to press, speak, release, with a small live indicator and direct insertion. Lesson: interaction should be understandable at a glance; the visual identity need not reproduce the interaction sequence. Do not copy its capsule/waveform language. Sources: [official Mac product page](https://aquavoice.com/mac), [Product Hunt interface capture](https://ph-files.imgix.net/173ba99a-be69-4ec8-86a6-3ccd199b6669.jpeg).
4. **VoiceInk** — The visible app architecture uses a conventional sidebar, history list and recording-oriented visual language; live text display and recorder styles are configurable. Lesson: users value control over whether live text is exposed, but a history-centric screen is not the right identity metaphor for Saytile. Do not copy its waveform and recording-history card language. Sources: [official settings docs](https://tryvoiceink.com/docs/settings-general), [interface capture](https://assets.st-note.com/img/1754310753-PvtMDwrXZWVH3hLk5mRen6U4.png?fit=bounds&height=2000&quality=85&width=2000).
5. **MacWhisper** — Its strength is a text-centric transcript editor with playback, search, timestamps and a collapsible settings/sidebar. It is primarily a file transcription/archive workflow, not an always-on single stream. Lesson: transcript typography and reading comfort matter more than audio ornament. Do not copy the multi-segment transcript/player visual system. Sources: [official site](https://www.macwhisper.com/), [editorial interface capture](https://cdn.mos.cms.futurecdn.net/iE7ntMiQddfbje5okaGkSX-1920-80.png), [independent product screenshots and notes](https://lifetimemac.com/apps/macwhisper).
6. **Granola** — Its notes workspace uses a quiet editorial hierarchy, soft color accents and a restrained navigation system. Lesson: make text the primary material and use color to organize attention rather than decorate every surface. Do not copy meeting-note hierarchy or folder metaphors. Sources: [official help / app guide](https://help.granola.ai/article/granola-101), [interface capture](https://prod-uk-services-workspac-workspacefilespublicbuck-vs4gjqpqjkh6.s3.amazonaws.com/w_01HKMWFY5JYE7CXFH8NH05XBGF/wFile_01K1ARYX6Q91228R6NFX48NXNG).

### Persistent desktop utilities and adjacent interaction models

7. **SideNotes** — Edge-resident writing space, quickly shown and hidden without breaking focus; restrained functionality and theme flexibility. Lesson: a narrow persistent surface can feel like a native part of the desktop when it respects focus and keeps controls secondary. Do not borrow the sticky-note metaphor. Sources: [official product listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sidenotes-screen-edge-notes/id1441958036?mt=12), [official screenshot](https://www.apptorium.com/public/image/articles/sidenotes/note-from-pasteboard/app.jpg).
8. **Dropover** — A floating shelf appears contextually and holds files until they are moved onward; recent versions support screenshot capture and sorting. Lesson: spatial persistence and transient surfaces can reduce context switching. Do not turn Saytile into a shelf, stack, or file container. Sources: [official 5.4 release](https://dropoverapp.com/whats-new/5.4.0), [official screenshot/help](https://dropoverapp.com/kb/take-screenshots), [editorial settings capture](https://images.sayzlim.net/2021/03/dropover-shelf-setting.webp).
9. **PopClip** — Its text-selection popover is small, highly contextual, and lets the selected content determine the available actions. Its settings preview communicates behavior with a real example instead of abstract decoration. Lesson: keep the primary surface quiet and reveal controls in context. Do not copy its floating action-strip shape. Sources: [official settings guide](https://www.popclip.app/guide/settings), [official settings screenshot](https://www.popclip.app/assets/shot-settings-general.D81dlp4o.png).
10. **Paste** — Its clipboard history uses clear content-type cues, typography and a floating panel that makes retrieval immediate. Lesson: content should remain legible and recognizable in compact surfaces. Do not copy the stack/list or clipboard visual metaphor. Sources: [official site](https://pasteapp.io/), [official interface image](https://pasteapp.io/_next/image?q=75&url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Fimage-1.0wawc96xxemjy.png&w=3840).
11. **Raycast** — A command launcher uses disciplined type hierarchy, dense but controlled spacing and one obvious primary input. Its screenshot/search features are integrated as commands rather than cluttering the base surface. Lesson: one strong focal point, carefully tuned density and clear hierarchy. Do not copy the command palette or its icon. Sources: [official redesign article](https://www.raycast.com/blog/the-new-raycast), [official screenshot documentation](https://manual.raycast.com/screenshots), [interface capture](https://cdn.cleanmymac.com/cdn-cgi/image/format%3Dauto%2Cformat%3Dpng%2Cquality%3D60%2Cwidth%3D1340%2Cdpr%3D2/blog_articles/May2026/Best%20reviewed%20Mac%20apps8.png).

### Premium consumer software and brand craft

12. **Craft** — A document workspace balances a strong editorial canvas with structured navigation and restrained, highly composed color accents. Lesson: visual warmth can coexist with information density when typography and spacing are disciplined. Do not copy the card-based dashboard or document icon. Sources: [official site](https://www.craft.do/), [interface capture](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize%3Afit%3A1400/0%2AO81tCSUJYRAyVaWX).
13. **mymind** — Uses a distinctive dark canvas, editorial search typography and a visually rich but low-friction collection grid. Lesson: brand identity can be atmospheric and distinctive without literal feature illustration; use negative space and image rhythm with intent. Do not copy the masonry collection layout or its orange/black mood. Sources: [official site](https://mymind.com/), [independent interface review](https://arslan.io/2023/01/30/a-review-of-a-mind-tool/).
14. **Linear** — Its identity system is deliberately consistent: monochrome logo assets, generous clear space, a restrained blue accent, and a disciplined typography system. Lesson: brand confidence comes from proportion, consistency and restraint—not from adding a concept to every surface. Do not copy the circular mark or indigo palette. Sources: [official brand guidelines](https://linear.app/brand), [desktop interface capture](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize%3Afit%3A1320/format%3Awebp/1%2A7JffQqVjvCxYqWra-oQK-A.png).
15. **Arc** — A browser that gives the sidebar and workspaces a strong spatial role, with controlled color and a recognizable overall silhouette. Lesson: desktop products can establish identity through a coherent spatial language. Do not copy the tab sidebar or gradients. Sources: [official site](https://arc.net/), [editorial interface capture](https://www.popsci.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/08/Arc-Browser-Tabs.jpeg?auto=webp&optimize=high&width=382).
16. **Things 3** — Uses typographic restraint, strong alignment, quiet grouping and a recognizably crafted icon. Lesson: everyday-use software benefits from calm surfaces and precise visual rhythm. Do not copy the checklist or calendar icon language. Sources: [official product site](https://culturedcode.com/things/), [official release notes](https://culturedcode.com/things/support/articles/1100684/), [interface capture](https://pnkjdm.files.wordpress.com/2023/07/cleanshot-2023-07-27-at-00.03.19402x.png?w=1024).

## What the visual evidence says

### Why simple marks become beautiful and memorable

Simplicity is not a low number of parts. It is a high degree of formal inevitability: silhouette, proportion, negative space and optical balance feel resolved; no part appears to exist only to explain a feature. The best small marks retain a distinctive outer contour before color or material is added. A shape that needs a paragraph of concept explanation is not yet a successful mark.

### Why a mark need not depict the feature

A mark becomes recognizable through repeated, consistent use and a distinct visual signature. The mark's job is to be ownable and emotionally appropriate; product comprehension is carried by name, onboarding, interface and context. Directly illustrating "voice to text" tends to produce the same microphone/wave/text glyphs as every competitor.

### Why desktop utilities look premium or cheap

Premium utilities are spatially disciplined: they have one clear focal point, a stable alignment system, intentional density, quiet controls, and predictable motion. Cheap utilities often overuse outlines, floating pills, gradients, status labels and generic glyphs to compensate for a weak underlying composition.

### Category conventions to avoid

- Microphone + waveform combinations.
- Three-bar equalizer marks.
- Chat bubbles, document sheets, stacked cards and generic windows.
- Generic AI stars, gradient orbs and glass blobs.
- Default S monograms without a distinctive custom construction.
- Overly thin line icons that fail at 16 px.
- App icons that merely screenshot the UI.

### What Saytile should learn

1. **Wispr Flow:** distinguish the brand mark from the transient control surface; keep the logo simple and stable.
2. **Superwhisper:** invest in a custom, sculptural silhouette rather than feature illustration; reject its particular loop motif as too close to an existing identity.
3. **SideNotes:** design for an always-available narrow panel without making it feel like a toolbar or sticky note.
4. **PopClip:** controls should be contextual and quiet.
5. **Linear / Things:** precision, clear space, typography and restraint create premium perception.
6. **mymind / Craft:** warmth and individuality can come from editorial color and spatial rhythm, not generic AI gradients.

## Design criteria for the next round

- Start from pure black/white silhouette.
- Three directions must differ in construction, not merely in metaphor or palette.
- Reject anything that reads as a microphone, waveform, text lines, note/page, card stack, window, AI star or generic S.
- Assess outer contour at 16/24/32/64 px before wordmark or color.
- The symbol must have a visually resolved negative space and optical center.
- The custom wordmark must be drawn or meaningfully modified; no default typeface set in lowercase.
- Only after a black/white winner exists should color be explored.
- Color must work in the narrow text-first panel without tinting large text surfaces.
- Brand symbol and status indicators are separate systems.
- Do not claim a design is approved merely because it is coherent or technically exportable.

## Apple platform constraint

Apple's current icon guidance specifies a 1024×1024 macOS icon canvas, system masking, centered primary content, simple distinctive shapes, and checking all appearances. Avoid pre-baked corner masks and decorative glows/shadows that fight system-rendered effects. See [Apple HIG — App icons](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons) and [Apple Design Resources](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/).
