Study the craft.
Not the category clichés.

A curated board of real product interfaces and official visual assets. Images are labelled by provenance. The goal is to study visual decisions—not to copy logos, colors, or interface layouts.

Product truth

One continuous expression surface.

Saytile is an always-available macOS voice-input workspace. Speech appears as one evolving transcript in a narrow, persistent desktop panel. The brand mark must stand on its own; the product UI stays transcript-first and almost logo-free.

04 / Research synthesis

What makes the best references work

1. Form before metaphor

A simple mark is successful when its silhouette, optical balance and negative space feel inevitable—not when every curve can be explained as a feature.

2. Product and brand are distinct

The logo establishes memory and personality. The UI explains the product. A brand mark should not be a tiny screenshot of the workflow.

3. Quiet precision reads premium

Stable alignment, deliberate density, restrained color, real typography and contextual controls outperform generic gradients and decorative status chrome.
