# Saytile Logo Design Revision — 2026-11-07

Status: supersedes the preliminary Sentence Fold recommendation in brand-concept.md. No concept in v0.1 is approved.

## User correction
The previous exploration was rejected as too ordinary and too focused on explaining product functions. The four supplied images are only slightly acceptable references, not approved artwork.

**A logo must first be a beautiful, concise, well-composed logo. It is not an infographic, product diagram, or a puzzle that must encode every product attribute.**

Do not try to depict voice + text + continuity + AI + space + persistence simultaneously. Do not explain the product through the logo.

## What is worth learning from the four references
1. Soft lilac/violet sculptural surface with a curved inner form: interesting material, contour, and inner/outer relationship. Do not copy the literal icon or add a cursor as a functional pictogram.
2. Folded continuous ribbon forming an S-like silhouette: the most promising formal reference because it has a simple memorable silhouette and strong visual rhythm. Do not make a generic S or stock folded-ribbon mark.
3. Symmetric purple bars: too close to generic equalizer/audio waveform; reject as a direct logo direction.
4. Open geometric frame with a soft curved inner plane: promising negative-space and open/closed contour relationship. Avoid becoming a generic window, note, or app-frame icon.

## New design hierarchy
1. Beauty and visual quality.
2. Silhouette, proportion, rhythm, balance, negative space.
3. Simplicity and recognizability at small sizes.
4. Distinctive character and ownability.
5. Wordmark quality and typographic fit.
6. Product relevance as a subtle secondary property.

The product relationship should be felt, not diagrammed.

## Required exploration method
- Start with the symbol alone, in black on white and white on black.
- Explore genuinely different formal constructions, not four colorways of one mark.
- Use few shapes, deliberate curves, strong silhouette, excellent optical balance.
- Explore ribbon/fold, open contour/inner plane, and sculptural negative-space approaches without literalizing them.
- No rounded-square app container during primary logo design. App icon adaptation comes only after the standalone mark is strong.
- No gradients, glass, glow, 3D, background waves, mockups, brand boards, taglines, or explanatory labels in the first evaluation sheet.
- Show 8–12 standalone black-and-white marks, each with consistent scale and generous whitespace. Include a small-size strip at 16/24/32 px after the large-form review.
- Do not generate app icons or a color system until one symbol direction wins on form alone.
- Evaluate the wordmark independently: custom letterform, proportion, and kerning—not a generic sans font typed beside an icon.

## Rejection criteria
Reject any mark that looks like a common microphone, equalizer, waveform, generic S, chat bubble, document, window, or AI logo; needs gradient/glow/shadow/container to look attractive; collapses at 16 px; resembles a small utility icon; or tries to explain too much.

## Product truth as a secondary constraint
Saytile is an always-available personal expression workspace. Speech forms one continuous, faithful, revisable transcript. The mark may subtly suggest continuity or a transition from fluid to ordered form, but must not depict the panel or technical architecture.

## Decision
The earlier A — The Sentence Fold and Persimmon recommendation are withdrawn as premature. Existing SVGs are disposable sketches only. No production asset, app icon, menu-bar icon, color, or wordmark is approved.


## Research correction after the user's rejection

The following primary-source references were examined:
- Wispr Flow's own 2025 rebrand case study: https://wisprflow.ai/rebrand
- Granola's own 2026 identity launch, including its collaboration with Ragged Edge: https://www.granola.ai/blog/a-new-look-for-granola
- Craft's official brand asset rules: https://www.craft.do/zh-TW/brand
- Apple's current App Icon HIG: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons/

### What the research changes
- Wispr's published system already uses cool blue/purple, organic fluid motifs, editorial layouts and a "Voice in Motion" idea. A generic purple folded ribbon or flowing S would risk looking derivative in this category. Do not use "voice in motion" or a similar river/ribbon concept as the default Saytile territory.
- Granola demonstrates that a strong identity can come from a distinctive hand-drawn symbol and a coherent typographic voice, not a literal feature illustration. The lesson is to seek a memorable authored contour, not to imitate its spiral or deliberately imperfect style.
- Craft's asset rules distinguish wordmark from standalone symbol and reserve the symbol for compact contexts. The lesson is to design the standalone mark as a complete object, with the wordmark treated as a separate typographic craft problem.
- Apple's HIG reinforces a simple, unique core shape and warns against fine detail or reproducing UI components. This supports form-first evaluation, not app-tile-first ideation.

### Updated creative direction
Do not start from the product's feature list, nor from a generic "voice-to-text" metaphor. Start from a strong authored silhouette and typographic character. Test:
1. Custom letterform / ligature construction using the name Saytile, without simply drawing a generic S.
2. Sculptural negative-space symbol with one memorable contour.
3. Restrained open/closed form relationship, only if it reads as a unique mark rather than a window or document icon.

The supplied user references are visual-quality references only. The first and fourth suggest nuanced surface/negative-space relationships; the second suggests contour rhythm but overlaps too closely with the fluid-ribbon territory already occupied by Wispr; the third equalizer bars are rejected as generic.

### Rejection of current generated sheets
The AI-generated black-and-white 12-mark sheet and subsequent purple brand boards are **not approved design deliverables**. They are generic, repetitive, and fail authorship/ownability. Do not place them in the official brand asset folder or use them as candidate finals. All earlier v0.1 SVG marks remain disposable sketches.

### New gate before visual production
First produce a research contact sheet of admired marks from the named reference brands and adjacent premium consumer software, with written formal analysis (silhouette, geometry, negative space, typographic relationship, distinctiveness). Then create a small set of bespoke Saytile marks in vector form and test them in black/white at 16/24/32/64 px. No gradients, color palette, mockups, or App Icon until one mark wins on silhouette alone.
