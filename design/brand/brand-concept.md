# Saytile Brand Concepts
Date: 2026-11-07 · Exploration set, not yet a production asset freeze.

## Four distinct creative territories

### A — The Sentence Fold (recommended)
A single continuous band passes through a deliberate fold and resolves into short, aligned typographic strokes. The fold represents the moment expression becomes legible structure; the aligned strokes represent one transcript that can keep extending and revising. It is neither an audio waveform nor a literal text-page icon.
- Geometry: one connected silhouette, asymmetric entry, central negative-space aperture, three unequal but optically aligned terminals.
- Strength: combines continuity and written form without illustrating a microphone or chat.
- Risk: if the terminals are too regular it becomes a generic text icon; if the fold is too fluid it becomes a waveform. Keep the fold architectural and the line endings typographic.

### B — Emergent Glyph
A compact modular glyph assembled from offset typographic fragments that lock into one coherent mark.
- Geometry: three interdependent masses, one intentional void, no enclosing rounded square.
- Strength: distinctive and highly scalable; expresses progressive formation.
- Risk: can read as a generic abstract technology logo unless the fragments share a recognizable reading order.

### C — Expression Aperture
An open spatial frame with a shifting inner plane; the mark describes a quiet place that remains available for expression.
- Geometry: asymmetrical aperture, interrupted boundary, one inner plane.
- Strength: strongest association with the persistent desktop workspace.
- Risk: can drift toward generic window, app-frame, or note-container symbolism; less explicit about spoken language becoming text.

### D — Voice Imprint
A layered, pressed ribbon leaves a stable typographic trace.
- Geometry: two interlocking planes and a controlled offset, creating depth through negative space rather than gradients.
- Strength: tactile, memorable, emotionally warm.
- Risk: can resemble a folded-paper or file-transfer product; depth may collapse at menu-bar size.

## Preliminary decision
A is the strongest strategic fit because it uniquely links the continuous nature of spoken expression with the ordered nature of a readable transcript. B is the strongest challenger for small-size distinctiveness. C is the strongest workspace metaphor but weaker product comprehension. D is visually expressive but risks communicating a generic file or note utility.

The mark must be tested in silhouette, at 16 px, in monochrome, and inside the actual 320–360 pt transcript panel before any final lock.
