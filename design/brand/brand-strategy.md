# Saytile Brand Strategy

## Product truth
Saytile is an always-available personal voice-input and expression workspace. It continuously captures speech when explicitly enabled, preserves original audio independently, and forms one faithful, revisable transcript. The user can read, edit, search, replay, copy, and deliberately reuse that text. The product is not a recorder, meeting bot, chatbot, note-taking canvas, or automatic writing polisher.

The defining transformation is not sound becoming a file. It is **expression becoming usable text, continuously, in a quiet persistent space**.

## Brand proposition
**Speak naturally. Keep your words in motion.**
Saytile gives spoken expression a persistent written presence without turning the user's desktop into another destination app.

## Brand pillars
1. Continuity — one evolving transcript, not disposable recordings or competing drafts.
2. Fidelity — preserve what the user meant and actually said; do not silently rewrite their voice.
3. Presence — always available, quiet when not needed, legible when active.
4. Agency — the user controls pause, editing, reuse, deletion, and external insertion.
5. Craft — a small interface deserves exceptional typography, proportion, motion, and reliability.

## Emotional register
Confident, intimate, composed, articulate, quietly alive. Never clinical-AI, playful mascot, surveillance-like, loud, or over-technical.

## Visual strategy
Build the identity around a custom typographic/expressive glyph: a continuous form that resolves into an ordered written structure. It must not be a literal microphone, waveform, chat bubble, AI star, generic S, stacked note cards, or miniature screenshot of the panel.

The app icon may be expressive and distinctive. The panel must remain text-first and neutral. Brand color is an identity accent, not a recording/recognition/error state signal.

## Product application rules
- Main panel: no permanent large wordmark or decorative brand illustration. Use the mark only where it improves orientation; the transcript begins at the first content line.
- Menu bar: monochrome template glyph, custom-optimized at 16/18 pt; no brand color dependency.
- App icon: recognizable silhouette at 16 px in monochrome and 1024 px in color.
- Website: editorial expression-led storytelling, not a generic AI dashboard or audio waveform hero.
- Motion: only meaningful transitions from incoming expression to stable text; never animate continuously while silent.

## Non-goals
Do not imply perfect transcription, autonomous message sending, meeting recording, or automatic prose rewriting. Do not expose internal ASR experts or GPT orchestration in consumer-facing identity.
