# Saytile Brand Guidelines — Exploration v0.1
Status: proposed identity direction; not a production freeze.

## Identity concept
The Sentence Fold: a continuous expressive form that passes through a fold and resolves into an ordered written trace. The brand stands for spoken expression becoming persistent, usable text.

## Wordmark
Use the custom wordmark exploration in lowercase `saytile` as a single word. Prefer a humanist grotesk with open counters and distinctive terminals; avoid default system typography as the final brand wordmark. Product UI continues using native system typography for platform fit. Do not force a serif/sans pairing into the app itself.

## Symbol
The symbol must remain a solid, legible silhouette. No tiny internal hairlines, no generic three-bar waveform, no enclosing square as the logo itself. The macOS icon container is a separate application-icon composition.

## Color
Recommended exploration: Persimmon `#E9654B`, Ink `#24272B`, Porcelain `#F7F4EF`. Use monochrome variants wherever platform conventions require it.

## Product interface
The panel is a reading/writing space, not a brand billboard:
- No large logo or product name in the panel header.
- Transcript is the first visual content.
- Mark may appear in menu bar and archive/settings context, not as a permanent decorative panel ornament.
- Use brand accent for identity and occasional selected actions only.
- Never map listening, recognition, pause, or error to the brand mark's shape.

## App icon
A full 1024 px master with a clear silhouette, strong optical centering, and a restrained depth strategy. Test at 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512 and 1024 px. Do not use wordmark or tiny detail.

## Menu bar
Template-style monochrome glyph. Supply black/white artwork and verify 16/18 pt logical sizes at 1x/2x. The shape is stable across listening, paused and error; status is shown with a separate indicator or menu label.

## Accessibility and export
All color pairings require contrast checks. Provide SVG master, PDF, PNG exports, app iconset, template menu-bar artwork, favicon, and Apple Touch Icon. Preserve a source file and export manifest. This exploration branch does not replace current production assets.
